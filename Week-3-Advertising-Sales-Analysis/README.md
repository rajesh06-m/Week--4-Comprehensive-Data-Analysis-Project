# Week 3 – Advertising and Sales Analysis

## Overview

This folder contains the advertising and sales analysis completed during Week 3 of the internship.

## Work Completed

- Analysed advertising expenditure and sales data.
- Examined the relationship between TV, Radio, Newspaper advertising and Sales.
- Performed correlation analysis.
- Conducted hypothesis testing.
- Identified important patterns and relationships.
- Interpreted the results from a business perspective.

## Key Findings

TV advertising showed the strongest positive relationship with sales, followed by Radio advertising. Newspaper advertising showed a comparatively weaker relationship with sales.

## Outcome

The analysis provided insights into how different advertising channels are associated with sales performance and demonstrated the application of statistical techniques in business data analysis.
